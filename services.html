Audit, Accounting & Business Consultancy Services in Lebanon

JMD Audit Firm provides professional auditing, accounting, bookkeeping, tax, financial reporting, financial statement analysis, company registration and business consultancy services for companies, organizations and entrepreneurs in Lebanon and the MENA region.

Audit & Assurance Services

Our audit services include external auditing, internal auditing and forensic audit. We help management and stakeholders understand financial information, strengthen internal controls, identify risks and improve financial governance.

Accounting & Bookkeeping

JMD Audit Firm provides accounting and bookkeeping support, payroll processing and organized financial records. Accurate bookkeeping creates a reliable foundation for financial reporting, tax compliance and management decisions.

Financial Statements & Financial Analysis

We assist businesses with financial statements, financial reporting and financial analysis, helping management understand profitability, liquidity, financial position, trends and business performance.

Company Registration & CCIA Support

We support entrepreneurs and businesses with company registration in Lebanon, business setup requirements and accounting and administrative preparation related to the Chamber of Commerce, Industry and Agriculture (CCIA).

Tax, NSSF & Compliance

Our services include taxation, NSSF and compliance support, helping businesses maintain organized records and meet applicable financial and regulatory obligations.

Financial & Business Consultancy

Our financial consultancy and business consultancy services provide practical analysis and advice for budgeting, financial decisions, investment considerations, risk management and sustainable business growth.
